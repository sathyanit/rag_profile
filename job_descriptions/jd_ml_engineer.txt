Senior Machine Learning Engineer

We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning Engineer to join our AI team. You will be responsible for designing, building, and deploying production-grade ML systems that power our core products.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement end-to-end ML pipelines from data ingestion to model deployment
- Build and maintain NLP models for text classification, entity recognition, and semantic search
- Develop MLOps infrastructure using MLflow, Kubeflow, or similar platforms
- Collaborate with data engineers to build scalable data pipelines
- Fine-tune large language models (LLMs) and transformer-based models (BERT, GPT)
- Write production-quality Python code with unit tests and documentation
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct technical code reviews
- Partner with product teams to translate business needs into ML solutions

Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience in machine learning engineering
- Proficiency in Python, TensorFlow, and PyTorch
- Experience with NLP, deep learning, and transformer architectures
- Hands-on experience with MLflow, Airflow, or similar ML workflow tools
- Strong knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS or GCP)
- Experience with vector databases (ChromaDB, Pinecone, or Weaviate)
- Solid understanding of RAG systems and LLM applications
- MS or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or related field preferred

Nice to Have:
- Experience with LangChain or similar LLM frameworks
- Published research papers in ML/NLP
- Experience with real-time streaming (Kafka, Spark Streaming)
- Contributions to open-source ML projects
