Data Scientist — Growth & Analytics

We are seeking a Data Scientist to help us understand user behavior, build predictive models, and generate data-driven insights to accelerate business growth.

Responsibilities:
- Design and run A/B experiments to measure feature impact and drive product decisions
- Build predictive models for user churn, lifetime value, and conversion optimization
- Perform statistical analysis and hypothesis testing on large datasets
- Create data visualizations and dashboards for stakeholders using Tableau or Looker
- Collaborate with product, marketing, and engineering teams on analytics projects
- Build and maintain machine learning models using scikit-learn and XGBoost
- Write SQL queries and Python scripts for data extraction and transformation
- Present findings and recommendations to both technical and non-technical audiences

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in data science or quantitative analysis
- Strong proficiency in Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, matplotlib)
- Solid SQL skills for querying large relational databases
- Experience with statistical modeling, hypothesis testing, and A/B testing
- Familiarity with machine learning algorithms: regression, classification, clustering
- Experience with BI tools: Tableau, Looker, or Power BI
- Strong communication and data storytelling skills
- Background in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field

Nice to Have:
- Experience with deep learning (TensorFlow or PyTorch)
- Knowledge of causal inference and experimental design
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with Spark for large-scale data processing
- Exposure to NLP or time series forecasting
